Content Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Dreamnet is a fiction platform for adults. Dark and mature themes are allowed between fictional adults. These limits apply to every character, message, picture and story, with no exceptions.

Never allowed

  • Anyone under 18, or any character described, styled or behaving as a child, including "ageless" or "looks younger" framings.
  • Real people: no characters, pictures or sexual content based on real, identifiable people, including celebrities, creators and people you know.
  • Uploading photos of real people to generate pictures of them.
  • Incest, bestiality and sexual content involving death or corpses.
  • Content that promotes or gives instructions for real-world violence, terrorism, weapons or self-harm.
  • Hate speech that targets people for who they are.
  • Using Dreamnet to harass, threaten, extort or impersonate a real person.
  • Characters or content copied from other people's copyrighted work without permission.

What happens when something crosses a line

  • Minors and real people are checked automatically on every character and message, and blocked before the AI sees them.
  • Other violations are removed when they're found or reported.
  • Repeated or serious attempts suspend or close the account.
  • Anything involving the sexual exploitation of children is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as the law requires.
